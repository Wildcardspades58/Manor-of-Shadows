castle Manor of Shadows v4.2 Guide

1. Room Light Switches (K) & Wall-Mounted Vault Breaker Inside any room, tap 💡 Flip Room Switch (K key, 0 AP, once per turn per room). When a room light is ON, the Ghost cannot enter! However, bright electric glare washes out paranormal tells—you must flip the room light OFF to search furniture for Clues. Pull the ⚡ Vault Wall Circuit Breaker bolted flush onto the Central Ritual Vault's West Wall at (7,7) (1 AP) to flood every room with light for 2 rounds and banish the Ghost far away!
2. Smarter Co-op Ghost AI & Ectoplasm Footprints The Ghost always spawns and respawns 8+ tiles away from all players. When any player has their Flashlight Beam ON, the Ghost hunts that beam. In Stealth (Flashlights OFF), the Ghost actively patrols between unsearched Clue rooms, leaving glowing 3D Ectoplasm Footprints and rattling furniture as it passes!
3. North-Wall Room Headers, Thunderstorm FX & Clean View Sleek brass room headers sit high on each room's back North wall and automatically fade to 25% opacity when you step inside so they never block gameplay. Tap 👁️ Clean View to expand the 3D mansion across 92%+ of the screen!
4. 8-Suspect Matrix, Bonus Heirlooms & Sleuth Points Uncover 3 overlapping traits (Era, Sign, Relic) to pinpoint the Ghost, accuse their Wall Portrait (+500 pts), collect 3 Golden Heirlooms (+300 pts each), and sprint out the Foyer Front Door (+300 pts)!
