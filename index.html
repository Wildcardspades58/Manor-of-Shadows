castle Manor of Shadows v4.0 Guide

1. Touch, Tablet & PC Controls Tap any glowing teal floor square in 3D to walk there, or tap directly on shaking 3D furniture, stairs, or wall portraits! You can also use the on-screen D-Pad or WASD / Arrow Keys.
2. Two-Story Mansion & On-Map Room Banners In Ravenwood Clocktower Estate, step onto the Grand Staircase or Service Stairs and press E (or tap the floating Stairs pill) to climb between 1F Ground and 2F Upper Floor! Use the top [1F / 2F] buttons to peek upstairs anytime.
3. 8-Suspect Overlapping Matrix & Bonus Heirlooms Suspects share 3 overlapping traits (Era, Elemental Sign, Haunted Relic). Finding 1 clue narrows to 4 suspects; 2 clues narrows to 2 suspects; all 3 clues pinpoint the exact Ghost! Also hunt for 3 Golden Heirlooms (+300 pts) and Pocket Gadgets!
4. Sleuth Points & Online Room Codes Earn points for searching props (+50), finding Secret Passages (+150), True Clues (+250), Golden Heirlooms (+300), High-Fiving another player in the same room (+100), and Accusing the Portrait (+500)!
